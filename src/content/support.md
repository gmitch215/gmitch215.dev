## Support the Work

Almost everything I build is open source and free to use, from a game engine in C to the TCP libraries that make Cloudflare Workers talk to the rest of the internet. Support keeps that going. It pays for the domains, the servers, and the hours that turn a wall I hit in a real project into a published tool other people can build on.

It also funds the thing I care about most: The Earth App, my long-term work against the loneliness and mental-health epidemic. That is the through-line behind all of it, from a Discord server I started as a kid to studying Computer Science and Psychology at Dartmouth, aimed squarely at helping people feel less alone.

If any of it has been useful to you, thank you. Every bit of support means I get to keep moving forward.
