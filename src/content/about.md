I show my passion for video games, computers, and software through the
development, modification, and release of platforms and projects that engage
people in a fun and proactive way. My favorite color is lime green.

My first programming experience started in late 2018, working with Drupal and PHP on various websites inside brightplum. My first solo, industry-grade work came in 2019 when I built my first Discord bot for servers I privately managed. Falling in love with programming, with a head start in block editors like [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu), foreshadowed a long and active path in software for me at age 11. I did more JavaScript with my first node module, [nodebook](https://nodebook.js.org), before spending my middle school and early high school years on Minecraft plugins and mods.

[Terminal Miner](https://github.com/gmitch215/Terminal-Miner), the first game I ever made in C++, is when I started branching out into all kinds of programming languages.

I have built a lot of different things, starting from Discord bots and moving into Minecraft plugins. Along the way I became immersed in a rich developer culture, and I still take in feedback at a high standard to keep the work good. I tried game development for a while, but it never really clicked, especially as I slowly stopped playing games. In this waking age of artificial intelligence, human skill and knowledge only sharpen the need for creativity in tech.

My high school years saw me shift toward an interest in Psychology and human connection. I still really enjoyed programming and was not ready to give it up. So I built [The Earth App](https://earth-app.com), my first long-term prototype for fighting the mental health and loneliness epidemic in our society.
