**The Earth App** is **live in the app stores at v1.0.3**, with four surfaces that each do one job: a Drupal 11 API, a Cloudflare Worker, a Nuxt web app, and a native iOS and Android app. Then I compiled the whole mission into one small app. **recess** says _"Go Outside."_ **No server. No account. No competition. No social graph.** Nothing is uploaded, and the points buy nothing, _because there is nothing to buy._ I grew up inside engagement mechanics, so I built the opposite on purpose.

::insight{icon="i-lucide-quote" title="What Two Summers Taught Me"}
"Developers have a problem-solving mindset; it becomes difficult for us to recognize when it shouldn't be us who solve the problem."
::

:::more{title="Aimed at the Problem"}
I taught for a second summer, and almost none of my plan survived. Java was too slow, so I pivoted to web design, then CodeCombat, then an Intro to AI unit. Half the class finished the final project, and half of those were pasted out of ChatGPT. I present that unsoftened, because the failure mode _is_ the research question.

::photo{src="/pictures/gregory-teaching.jpg" caption="Chicago State University, summer 2026. Thirty students, three pivots, and a curriculum I rewrote twice on the way."}
::

My interest in mental health is _not academic_. I am the same self-aware, easily drained, wants-to-be-included kid. That is why I am majoring in **Computer Science at Dartmouth with a Quantitative Social Science minor**, keeping psychology for its research methods. I moved into Hanover on **September 3** and kept shipping. I call myself the world's okayest developer. It is a good joke, and it is _wrong_. I learned ten languages because I _had a reason to_.
:::
