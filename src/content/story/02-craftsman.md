The gap between 2021 and 2022 is the largest single-year leap in the record. My commit count went up _fivefold_, my Maven plugins became multi-module **Gradle Kotlin DSL** builds, and **MobChip**, an Entity AI Library for SpigotMC, reached **88 stars** across 17 versioned adapters. After I stopped maintaining it, another developer took it over, which is about the highest compliment an open-source library gets.

:::more{title="What 2022 Added Up To"}
Novaconomy grew a **25-version NMS abstraction layer**: reflection-based adapters for every Minecraft server release from _1.8 to 1.20_. Minecraft's internals are obfuscated and change every version, and abstracting across 25 of them defeats plenty of professional plugin developers.

MobChip has **35 test files**, CI, a Discord community and a Maven repository other developers depended on. Beside it came the **Big 4**: Novaconomy, StarCosmetics, PlasmaEnchants and BattleCards. By the end of the year a _fourteen-year-old_ maintained several published libraries, with tests and versioned releases, that strangers used.
:::
