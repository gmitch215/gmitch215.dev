Three storylines converge in 2025. **cmdfx** is a game engine for the terminal, written from scratch in **C**, because after **kasciffy** I needed an excuse to get back into C. I taught **Java to seventh through ninth graders** that summer. And in _May_ I founded the org behind **The Earth App**, a platform against loneliness, which won the **2025 Congressional App Challenge** for Illinois' 2nd District.

:::more{title="The Three Storylines"}
cmdfx has a CMake build, sanitizer harnesses, a hand-rolled assertion library and installers for Windows, macOS and Linux. I used to call it my clean control sample, and it is not. My editor's chat history puts conversational AI in the project from **April 2025**, so 298 of its 465 commits predate any assistance. The real control sample is bigger: **6,850 commits predate my first chat prompt**, and **9,683 predate any agentic tool**.

The Earth App began as a five-language microservices system. Its TypeScript backend on Hono was _too slow_ paired with CollegeDB, the sharding router I had pulled out of it, so I threw out 239 commits and took my father's one recommendation: **Drupal**. That is the only technical advice from him in this story, accepted after a measured failure, and it is the seed that grew into Drupflare.

I am easily drained, and more than anything I want to feel _included_. In 2025 I decided to point my one real superpower at the thing that hurts.
