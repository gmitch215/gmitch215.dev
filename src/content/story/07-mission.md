My interest in mental health is _not academic_. I am that same self-aware, easily-drained, wants-to-be-included kid, deciding to point my one real superpower at the thing that hurts. That is why I am pairing **Computer Science with Psychology at Dartmouth** and aiming both at the problem, rather than at a job title.
