"Go Outside." A few small prompts a day, drawn from a catalog of 120 and filtered by clock, season, weather, and what you have already done. No server, no account, no competition, no social graph. An on-device CLIP model, sentence embedder, and Whisper check the nudges; nothing is uploaded, ever, and the points buy nothing because there is nothing to buy.
