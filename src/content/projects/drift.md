Why does it pass here and fail there? Drift captures an environment as a hashed capsule, ranks what differs between a passing run and a failing one, proposes the experiment that removes the most uncertainty, and re-derives every verdict from the archived case. Kotlin Multiplatform, with a portability atlas of 38 programs recorded on 13 Kotlin targets.
