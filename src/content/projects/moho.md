The calendar under The Earth App's events. Reads seven date formats, from fixed dates through relative ones ("3rd Monday in January") to astronomical cycles like the saros and the Western and Orthodox moveable feasts. The v1.1.0 release regenerated its data from Wikidata at day precision, dropped the rows that were wrong, and added a checked-in fact snapshot the test suite asserts on every run.
